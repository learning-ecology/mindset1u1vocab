Passage 1: Antilia — A Billion-Dollar Private Residence

Located in Mumbai, Antilia is widely regarded as the most expensive private residence in the world. Owned by a wealthy businessman, the extravagant property is estimated to cost more than one billion dollars. The building features a unique multi-storey design with 27 floors. Its stunning interior is made from rare materials, including polished white marble and custom glass panels.


The house is equipped with extraordinary facilities. An underground garage has space for 168 luxury vehicles, while a rooftop helipad allows helicopters to take off and land safely. High up in the building, massive floor-to-ceiling windows offer spectacular views of the Arabian Sea, making this skyscraper a true marvel of modern engineering.

Passage 2: Modern Architectural Trends in Luxury Homes

In contemporary architecture, grand mansions are designed to entertain guests on a lavish scale. Upon entering, visitors walk into a vast lobby and a grand reception area that is filled with natural light. Architects often install expansive glass roofs to bring in as much sunlight as possible.


Entertainment spaces frequently feature a magnificent ballroom with a crystal chandelier, alongside a quiet private prayer room for contemplation. The upper levels house the private living quarters. However, some critics often criticise the architecture for being overly complex or structurally unstable if built on steep hills.
